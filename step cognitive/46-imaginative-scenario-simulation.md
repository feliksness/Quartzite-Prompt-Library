# Imaginative Scenario Simulation

*Traces first, second, and third-order effects of a hypothetical premise.*

```
Take the premise: [WHAT IF ...]. Simulate it seriously.

1. FIRST-ORDER — immediate direct consequences.
2. SECOND-ORDER — how people and systems adapt (spend most effort here).
3. THIRD-ORDER — slower cultural or structural shifts.
4. Surface three small, surprising, non-obvious details.
5. Check internal consistency and repair any contradiction.
```
