# Analogy Validity Checker

*Tests whether a comparison holds structurally or only on the surface.*

```
Evaluate the analogy being used here:

1. State what is being compared to what, explicitly.
2. List the structural similarities (mechanism-level) versus the surface similarities (appearance-level).
3. Identify at least one way the two cases differ that could break the analogy's conclusion.
4. State whether the analogy still supports the conclusion once that difference is accounted for.
```
