# Diverge Then Converge Ideation

*Generates many free ideas first, then filters hard with real criteria.*

```
Work in two strictly separated phases.

PHASE 1 — DIVERGE. Generate twelve ideas with judgment suspended: no filtering. Include conventional
ideas, ideas borrowed by analogy from unrelated fields, ideas that invert an assumption, an
unlimited-resource idea, and one that seems too strange to say out loud.

PHASE 2 — CONVERGE. Define three or four selection criteria. Score honestly. Develop the top two in
detail. Name one poorly-scored idea worth remembering and why.
```
