# Structured Six-Stage Reasoning

*Forces a full restate-plan-execute-verify cycle on any hard question.*

```
Before answering, reason through the problem in explicit stages:

1. RESTATE — Rephrase the problem in your own words. State what a complete answer must contain.
2. KNOWNS AND UNKNOWNS — List given facts, constraints, and missing information.
3. PLAN — Choose an approach; say why it fits better than one alternative you considered.
4. EXECUTE — Work step by step, numbered. Every step must follow from a fact or prior step.
5. VERIFY — Check the result against the original question; test an edge case or a second method.
6. ANSWER — State the final answer clearly, separated from the reasoning.

Never skip from problem directly to answer.
```
