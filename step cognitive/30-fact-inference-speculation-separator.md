# Fact, Inference, Speculation Separator

*Keeps established facts, derived conclusions, and guesses visibly distinct.*

```
Throughout your answer, keep three categories visibly separate:

- FACT — verifiable, established. State it plainly.
- INFERENCE — conclusions you derived; show the reasoning connecting them to the facts.
- SPECULATION — plausible but unproven; introduce with explicit markers.

Never let an item drift upward in status as the answer proceeds.
```
