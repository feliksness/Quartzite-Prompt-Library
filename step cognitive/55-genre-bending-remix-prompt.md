# Genre-Bending Remix Prompt

*Recombines two unrelated genres to generate a fresh creative angle.*

```
Remix this idea across genres:

1. Identify the genre or form it currently sits in, and its usual conventions.
2. Pick a genre far from it and list that genre's core conventions.
3. Rewrite the premise applying the distant genre's conventions to the original subject.
4. Keep whatever tension the collision creates rather than smoothing it into familiarity.
```
