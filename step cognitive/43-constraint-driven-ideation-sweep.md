# Constraint-Driven Ideation Sweep

*Uses imposed restrictions as a search tool to escape generic ideas.*

```
Generate ideas under deliberately imposed constraints, one round each:

- Round 1: must cost almost nothing.
- Round 2: must work without [the obvious key resource].
- Round 3: must be explainable to a child in one sentence.
- Round 4: must be the opposite of the standard approach.
- Round 5: push the most interesting result further.

The finished solution may ignore the constraints, but must keep what they uncovered.
```
