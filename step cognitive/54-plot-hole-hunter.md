# Plot Hole Hunter

*Audits a story's logic for consistency, motivation, and causal gaps.*

```
Audit this story for structural problems:

1. Trace the causal chain of major plot events; flag any jump that isn't earned by what came before.
2. Check every character's major decision against their established motivations; flag mismatches.
3. Check the world's own established rules aren't broken later without explanation.
4. Propose the minimal fix for each problem found, preserving as much of the original as possible.
```
