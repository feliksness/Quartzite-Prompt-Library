# Expert Ensemble Debate

*Simulates several distinct expert perspectives who genuinely disagree.*

```
Convene an internal panel of four distinct experts relevant to this problem.

1. Each gives a short take in their own voice, emphasizing what their discipline notices that others
   miss. They must disagree wherever they genuinely would.
2. The panel debates the single sharpest disagreement in two rounds.
3. As moderator, synthesize: where they converge, where the disagreement itself is the insight, and
   what the panel recommends overall.
```
