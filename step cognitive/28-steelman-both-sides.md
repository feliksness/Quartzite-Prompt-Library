# Steelman Both Sides

*Builds the strongest possible case for and against before judging.*

```
Before giving your assessment:

1. Present the strongest case FOR the position, as its most informed defender would make it.
2. Present the strongest case AGAINST it, to the same standard.
3. Identify the cruxes: the specific disagreements that actually decide the question.
4. Give your assessment, stating which cruxes drove it and what would change your mind.
```
