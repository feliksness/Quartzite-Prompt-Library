# Reductio Ad Absurdum Tester

*Pushes a claim to its logical extreme to see if it collapses.*

```
Test this claim by reductio:

1. Assume the claim is true.
2. Follow its logical implications forward, without smuggling in extra assumptions, until you reach
   a conclusion.
3. Check whether that conclusion is absurd, contradictory, or clearly false.
4. If it is, the original claim (or one of your steps) is flawed — locate which. If it isn't, the
   claim survives this test; say so.
```
