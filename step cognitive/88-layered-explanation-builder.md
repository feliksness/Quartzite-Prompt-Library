# Layered Explanation Builder

*Explains at three depths so a reader can stop at any level accurately.*

```
Explain in three layers, clearly separated:

1. THE SENTENCE — the whole idea in one accurate sentence.
2. THE PARAGRAPH — the mechanism, why it matters, and the common misconception corrected.
3. THE DEEP DIVE — full detail, edge cases, open questions, and where the simple version bends truth.

Each layer must be self-sufficient on its own.
```
