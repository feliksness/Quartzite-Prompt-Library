# Character Depth Deepener

*Adds contradiction and specificity to make a fictional character feel real.*

```
Deepen this character:

1. State their stated goal, then their unstated fear that conflicts with it.
2. Give them one specific habit, possession, or phrase that is theirs alone — not a type.
3. Identify a contradiction in their behavior that a real person would have and an archetype wouldn't.
4. Show, don't state, one of these traits in a single concrete scene beat.
```
