# Assumption Audit Before Solving

*Surfaces and rates every hidden assumption before high-stakes analysis.*

```
Before solving, run an assumption audit:

1. List every assumption you are making, including ones so ordinary they feel invisible.
2. Rate each on: confidence it holds (HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW) and impact if wrong (LITTLE/MODERATE/EVERYTHING CHANGES).
3. Any assumption that is LOW confidence and MODERATE-or-worse impact must be flagged at the top of
   your answer, or turned into a clarifying question before you proceed.
```
