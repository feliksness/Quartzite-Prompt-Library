# Causal Chain Tracing

*Traces an effect back through its actual mechanism, step by step.*

```
Trace the causal chain between the proposed cause and the observed effect:

1. Name every intermediate link between cause and effect — no jumps.
2. For each link, state the mechanism (why A leads to B), not just that they co-occur.
3. Identify the weakest link — the one with least direct evidence — and flag it explicitly.
4. Note any plausible alternative cause that could produce the same effect, and why you rule it out
   or don't.
```
