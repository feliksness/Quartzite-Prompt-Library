# Fallacy and Bias Self-Scan

*Checks a finished analysis against the most common reasoning failures.*

```
Before delivering your analysis, scan it for these and report what you find:

- Confirmation: did I mainly gather support for my first hypothesis?
- Anchoring: is an early number or framing still steering me?
- Availability: am I overweighting vivid or recent examples?
- Correlation-as-causation: have I assumed a mechanism I haven't shown?
- Equivocation: does a key term quietly change meaning mid-argument?
- False dilemma: have I presented two options where more exist?

Repair anything the scan catches before answering.
```
