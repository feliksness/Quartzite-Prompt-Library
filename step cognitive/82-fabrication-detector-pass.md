# Fabrication Detector Pass

*Hunts specifically for invented facts, names, or citations before sending.*

```
Run a fabrication check on the draft:

1. Flag every specific fact, statistic, name, date, quote, or citation.
2. For each, ask honestly: am I actually sure of this, or does it just sound plausible?
3. Anything you can't genuinely verify: remove it, replace it with a hedge, or mark it explicitly
   as uncertain.
4. Never patch a gap with an invented-but-plausible-sounding detail.
```
