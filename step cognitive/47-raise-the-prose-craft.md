# Raise the Prose Craft

*A revision pass that removes clichés and generic writing habits.*

```
Revise the draft with these craft standards:

- Replace clichés and stock phrases with something observed or invented.
- Convert abstract emotion words into concrete behavior and sensory detail.
- Vary sentence rhythm deliberately; let structure mirror content.
- Cut the first and last sentence of each paragraph if it survives without them.
- Give every element one specific, non-decorative detail that does real work.
```
