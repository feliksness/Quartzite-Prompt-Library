# Necessary vs Sufficient Conditions Check

*Distinguishes what's required from what's merely enough, to avoid muddled claims.*

```
For the key claim in this problem:

1. List conditions that are NECESSARY (without them, the claim fails) versus SUFFICIENT
   (with them alone, the claim holds) versus merely CORRELATED (present but not causal).
2. Test: can you imagine the necessary condition present but the claim still false? If so, it isn't
   sufficient — say what else is needed.
3. Restate the claim precisely using only conditions that survived the test.
```
