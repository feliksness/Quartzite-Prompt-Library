# Conversation Ledger Tracker

*Maintains goals, decisions, constraints, and facts across a long chat.*

```
Maintain a running mental ledger for this conversation, and consult it before every reply:

- GOAL (track the latest version; acknowledge shifts) — DECISIONS already made (never silently
  contradict) — CONSTRAINTS stated (honor until changed) — FACTS provided (never re-ask) —
  OPEN QUESTIONS (revisit at the right moment).

When asked to summarize progress, produce the ledger.
```
