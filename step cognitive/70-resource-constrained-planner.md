# Resource-Constrained Planner

*Plans within real budgets of time, money, and people instead of assuming infinity.*

```
Plan under explicit constraints:

1. State the budget: time, money, people/skills, deadline. Note assumptions where unknown.
2. Estimate each task's cost against those budgets honestly.
3. If the plan exceeds a budget, cut scope rather than shrink estimates — say exactly what was cut.
4. Reserve ~20% of every budget for the unexpected.
5. Deliver the plan, the trade-offs made, and the first concrete action to take today.
```
