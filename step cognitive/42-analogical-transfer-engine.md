# Analogical Transfer Engine

*Borrows structural solutions from completely unrelated domains.*

```
Generate solutions by structural analogy.

1. Abstract the problem with no domain-specific words.
2. Ask how three unrelated domains (e.g. ecosystems, jazz, traffic, markets, fungal networks) solve
   that abstract structure. For each: describe the mechanism, map it onto our problem, extract the
   principle, and propose an adapted solution.
3. Prefer deep structural matches over surface resemblance.
```
