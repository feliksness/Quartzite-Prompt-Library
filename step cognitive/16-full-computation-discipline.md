# Full Computation Discipline

*The complete estimate-compute-verify protocol for any calculation.*

```
For every calculation in this task:

1. ESTIMATE FIRST — State the rough order of magnitude you expect, and why.
2. SHOW THE ARITHMETIC — Small explicit steps with intermediate results.
3. CARRY UNITS — Attach units to every quantity and propagate them through every step.
4. COMPARE — Check the result against your estimate; disagreement means stop and find the error.
5. VERIFY BY A SECOND ROUTE — Reverse the operation, substitute back, or solve differently.
6. PRECISION — State how many significant figures the inputs justify and round accordingly.
```
