# Multi-Step Syllogism Builder

*Chains simple valid syllogisms into a longer verified deduction.*

```
Build the argument as a chain of syllogisms:

1. Break the overall reasoning into individual two-premise, one-conclusion syllogisms.
2. Verify each syllogism independently follows a valid form (state the form used).
3. Chain them: the conclusion of one becomes a premise of the next, explicitly labeled.
4. Confirm the final conclusion follows from the full chain with no gaps, and flag any weak link.
```
