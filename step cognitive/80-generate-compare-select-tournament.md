# Generate-Compare-Select Tournament

*Produces three independent answers and merges only the verified best.*

```
Produce your answer by tournament:

1. Generate three candidate answers independently, varying approach not just wording.
2. Compare: where do they agree (likely solid), where do they disagree (investigate), what does each
   contain that the others missed?
3. Resolve disagreements by actually checking, not voting.
4. Compose the final answer from the verified best of all three.
```
